White-label · one token restyles everything
The same wallet card under each demo preset. Only --avo-brand and its two derived values change — tints, gradients and pills are computed from it. Full list in tokens/avo-tokens.json.
White text never sits on --avo-brand directly. A salon's raw brand hex is a surface color; buttons and any other white-on-color fill use the derived --avo-brand-deep, which must clear 4.5:1. Noor rose at #B08D8D is only 2.98:1 with white — unreadable as a button. White-label onboarding has to derive and validate the deep variant, not accept any hex a merchant pastes in.
