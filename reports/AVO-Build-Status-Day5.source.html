Now the constraint

What is blocking launch is no longer code

Fourteen open items need an AVO decision

Each is recorded with its reasoning and a safe default already shipped, so nothing is stalled today. But several have lead times measured in weeks, and they are now the long pole. The four with the longest lead are below.

1 Central Bank position on holding stored value Does AVO holding customer balances need its own licence? Our payment provider being licensed answers who moves money, not who may hold it. Regulatory, and the single largest launch risk.
2 Legal sign-off on the policy set Seven documents are written, versioned and served to the apps, each carrying “awaiting counsel and payment-provider sign-off”. Seeding the text is not approval of it.
3 WhatsApp templates and a sending domain Receipts, reset links and campaigns are fully built behind a logging driver — the queue, retries and de-duplication all work. Nothing can actually send until templates are approved.
4 Arabic review by a native speaker 74 strings have no Arabic source in the design bundle. They are tracked and deliberately left in English rather than machine-translated; a wrong register in a women’s salon product is a defect. The list is ready as a worksheet.

Also outstanding and lower risk: one hour with a physical phone to prove a live QR charge (the simulator has no camera), a decision on data residency, and who staffs the support queue and in what hours.

Assessment

Where this lands

On engineering alone, this build would be launch-ready well inside the 30 days. The realistic launch date is now set by the regulatory answer and the legal review, not by remaining development. The most valuable thing that can happen this week is starting items 1 and 2 — both have external lead times, and both can run in parallel with the hardening work already underway.
